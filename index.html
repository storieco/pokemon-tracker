Buy plan

Enter a budget and the plan splits it across the top-scoring products for the store type picked below (scores of 60 and up). Products that don't suit those stores are left out (no booster boxes or ETBs for counters). Better scores get a bigger share, and no single product gets more than 30%. Unit cost starts at market price; if the warehouse pays less, lower the percentage.

Cost & margin calculator

Pick a product from the drop-down to see everything the tracker knows about it: prices, buyer opinion, print risk, its buy score broken into all seven factors, and the forecast. Then type in what the warehouse pays per unit, how many units, the margin you want and what you plan to sell it for. Every box is editable. It shows the price you need to hit your margin, what the product sells for now and later, and how much room the store has left. Numbers here reset when the page reloads.

What to buy, ranked

Every product is scored out of 100 on seven factors. Hobby shops want booster boxes and ETBs. Gas stations and vape shops sell from the counter, so cheap packs, blisters and bundles fit them best. "Whole route" weighs both equally.

Price forecast

Where each product's market price is likely headed in 30 and 90 days. It follows the usual Pokémon pattern: new products start high, slide for a few months, level off, then slowly climb once out of print. Buyer opinion and overprint risk tilt it, and each product's own price history counts more as daily prices build up. Treat it as a guide, not a guarantee.
